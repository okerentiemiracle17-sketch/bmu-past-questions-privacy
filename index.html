Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

BMU Past Questions is an independent educational study platform developed and operated by Miracle Nexus. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Independence notice: BMU Past Questions is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or an official application of Bayelsa Medical University.

Information we process

  • Account and profile information such as name, email address, programme, academic level, profile picture and account identifiers.
  • Authentication, installation and device-session information used to secure accounts and enforce account/device rules.
  • Study activity needed for question practice, theory grading, Study Groups, results, usage limits and AI study features.
  • Documents, images, text and other study material you choose to upload or submit.
  • Push-notification tokens needed to deliver app and school-information notifications.
  • Payment references, product identifiers and subscription/entitlement status needed to verify and manage Premium access.
  • Advertising-related information that may be processed by Google AdMob according to Google policies, device settings and applicable consent requirements.
  • AI prompts, source materials and generated results when you use Nexus AI.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide study features, enforce usage limits, deliver notifications, operate Study Groups and Nexus AI, process uploaded learning material, verify Google Play purchases, prevent abuse, investigate reports, troubleshoot the service and maintain security.

Service providers

The app may use Supabase for authentication, database, storage and server functions; Firebase for push messaging; Google AdMob for advertising; Google Play Billing for Android digital purchases; YouTube for embedded educational videos; and configured AI providers to generate or assess educational content. Data is shared with these providers only as needed to provide the requested feature or operate the service.

Payments

Android Premium purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. BMU Past Questions does not store your card number, banking password, OTP or card PIN. We may retain a purchase token/reference and entitlement status needed to verify a transaction, prevent duplicate activation, provide support and manage Premium expiry.

Nexus AI

Prompts, uploaded source material and related content submitted to Nexus AI may be sent to configured AI providers to generate explanations, questions or grading feedback. AI output may contain mistakes and is a study aid, not guaranteed academic, medical or clinical advice. The app includes a report feature so users can flag offensive, dangerous, inappropriate or misleading AI-generated content for review.

User-generated content and moderation

Users may upload study materials and may appear in Study Group participant areas. Only material you are permitted to use should be submitted. Illegal, sexually explicit, hateful, harassing, abusive, deceptive, dangerous, infringing or otherwise objectionable content is prohibited. Shared uploads are subject to review before appearing in the common library. The app provides controls to report shared material or participants and to block participants where community interaction is shown. Reports may result in content removal or account restrictions.

Security

We use authenticated access, encrypted transport, row-level database security, protected storage rules, server-side verification for sensitive operations and secure backend functions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Account-linked information is retained while needed to operate and secure the service, support transactions, investigate abuse or satisfy legal obligations. When an account is deleted, data under our control is deleted except information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes. Any retained information is kept only for the period required for that purpose.

Your choices

You may update supported profile information, manage notification permissions through your device settings, choose whether to upload study material, report AI/UGC issues, block participants in supported community areas, and request permanent account deletion.

Children

The service is designed for university-level study and is not intended as a child-directed service. Users should use the service only where permitted by applicable law and applicable age requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, its providers or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material revisions are published.

Account & Data Deletion

Users can permanently delete their BMU Past Questions account and associated app data.

In-app deletion path
My Profile → Delete Account → Confirm permanent deletion

Deletion removes the BMU Past Questions account and associated data under our control, including profile information, device registration, app/study records linked to the account, uploaded user material and subscription records, except information that must be retained temporarily for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or payment-dispute purposes.

Can't access the app?

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app. Email the privacy contact below from the email address associated with your BMU Past Questions account and request permanent deletion of your account and associated data. We may verify account ownership before processing the request.
