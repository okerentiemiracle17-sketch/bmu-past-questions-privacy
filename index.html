Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

BMU Past Questions is an independent educational study platform developed and operated by Miracle Nexus. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Independence notice: BMU Past Questions is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or an official application of Bayelsa Medical University.

Information we process

  • Account and profile information such as name, email address, programme, academic level, profile picture and account identifiers.
  • Authentication and device/session information used to secure accounts and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Study and app activity needed for question practice, study groups, results, usage limits and AI study features.
  • Study materials, documents, images or text you choose to upload or submit.
  • Push-notification tokens needed to deliver notifications.
  • Payment references and subscription status needed to verify and manage Premium access. Payment-card credentials are handled by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored directly by BMU Past Questions.
  • Advertising-related information that may be processed by advertising services according to their policies and applicable consent requirements.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide educational features, manage subscriptions and usage limits, deliver notifications, operate Study Groups and Nexus AI, process uploaded learning materials, verify payments, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service and maintain security.

Service providers

The app relies on third-party services for cloud authentication/database/storage, notifications, advertising, payment processing and AI processing. Information may be transmitted to these providers as needed to provide the relevant feature.

Nexus AI

Prompts, source material or related content submitted to AI features may be processed by configured AI service providers to generate responses. AI output can contain errors and should not be treated as guaranteed academic, medical or clinical advice.

Security and retention

We use technical controls designed to protect user information. Account-linked information is retained while needed to operate the service and for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or legal purposes. When an account is deleted, associated data under our control is deleted except information that must be retained for a legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory reason.

Children

Users should use the service only where permitted by applicable law and applicable age requirements. Contact us if you believe a child's personal information has been provided contrary to applicable requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, its providers or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material revisions are published.

Account & Data Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account inside BMU Past Questions:

My Profile → Delete Account → Confirm permanent deletion

Deletion removes the BMU Past Questions account and associated user data under our control, subject only to information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory reasons.

Can't access the app?

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app. Email the privacy contact below from the email address associated with your BMU Past Questions account and request permanent deletion of your account and associated data.

Request Account Deletion

For security, we may verify that the requester controls the account. Never send your password, OTP, card PIN or banking credentials.
